Dear Kiki, ♡

Do you remember the first day you came to our school?

Because I definitely do. 😭

You were alone, going upstairs to your class, while I was coming down the stairs with some of my friends.

And when I saw you, I genuinely thought:

“Arey, ye toh ekdum bachchi lag rahi hai. Ye toh Class 5 ya 6 mein hogi. New hai, pakka.” 😭💀

You were wearing that sky-blue floral frock with jeans and sandals, your spectacles, and your ponytail.

You looked so decent and innocent that I was completely convinced you were some tiny new kid. 😭

And then later, after assembly, I went upstairs to my class and saw you sitting there.

In my class. 💀

I was like… BRO. 😭

Turns out you were actually my classmate. And looking back now, that first impression was absolutely hilarious because after getting to know you, I discovered something very important:

You were NOT innocent. 💀

Like, girl, the character development was insane. 😭

Anyway, on your first, second, and third day, we didn't really know each other properly.

We were basically just two people existing in the same classroom.

And then somehow, we ended up sitting on the same desk.

And that's literally where everything started.

First we became friends. Then we started talking more, spending more time together, trusting each other more, sharing random things, laughing over the dumbest stuff...

And slowly, without even realizing when it happened — we became best friends. ♡

And honestly? That's kinda crazy when you think about it.

A girl I initially thought was a Class 5 kid somehow became my best friend. 😭

Then came Class 9, and you had to leave for Jamia.

And that was a BIG blunder for me. 😭

You were the one person I could do practically everything with during classes.

When you left, everything suddenly felt really different.

I felt really left out after that, and I genuinely missed having you around.

Even now, whenever you come here and I get to meet you, I get SO happy. 🥹💗

Sometimes I'm busy and can't pick up your calls, but that doesn't mean I don't remember you.

I still miss you, and whenever we actually get to talk or meet, it makes me happy.

One thing I really like about you is that whenever I'm sad and talk to you, you always try to make me feel better.

Although...

I have one serious complaint. 😭

You somehow manage to get EVERYTHING out of me.

I'll literally think, “Nope. I'm not telling her this.”

And somehow five minutes later—

I'm telling you everything. 💀

Like HOW???

But okay, I admit it. You know me pretty well.

Although there are still plenty of things you don't know about me...

And some secrets are staying classified information. 🤭

One memory I still remember is our Class 9 one-day trip.

Honestly, calling it a “trip” is already being generous because our school really did the absolute bare minimum. 😭

But somehow, we still had SO much fun.

You made chicken noodles, we ate so many things, and we ate so much that when we finally got home, we didn't even need lunch. 💀

It wasn't some fancy trip.

It wasn't even the best-organized trip.

But I still remember it because we had so much fun that day. ♡

And there's one thing I want to say:

Bas khush reh aur jo goals hain, woh complete kar.

I don't know what the future is going to look like. Maybe we'll be busy, maybe we'll be far away, maybe we'll talk less sometimes.

But I really hope that no matter how much life changes, we'll still randomly text each other:

“Hi, kaisi ho?”

“Thik hoon, tum kaisi ho?”

Because that's just us. ♡

We may not sit on the same desk anymore. We may not spend every day together like we used to.

But somehow, we're still here.

Happy Birthday, Kiki! 🎂💗

Bas khush reh, apne goals complete kar, aur aise hi reh.

And please continue pretending you're innocent even though we both know the truth. 💀😭

Love you, bestie. ♡

— Your best friend 💌
